# Surplus2Serve - Live Demo Instructions

This document is the companion guide to `presentation_script.md`. It provides the exact "Director's Notes" on where to click, scroll, and point while delivering your presentation.

**Pre-requisites:**
1.  Open the app in your browser (`http://localhost:8080`).
2.  Ensure you have a **Donor** account and an **NGO** account ready (or know the credentials to type quickly).
3.  Have the `presentation_script.md` open on a second screen or printed out.
